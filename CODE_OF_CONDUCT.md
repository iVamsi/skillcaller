# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume the person you are replying to is acting in good faith and is short on
time.

## What is expected

- Keep discussion on the technical question at hand.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change, and that contributors may decline to make one.
- Respect that people here have different backgrounds, first languages, and levels of experience.

## What is not welcome

- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments.
- Harassment of any kind, in issues, pull requests, or private contact arising from this project.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and
anywhere someone represents the project publicly.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately through
[GitHub's report form](https://github.com/iVamsi/skillcaller/security/advisories/new) or by
contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are read by the maintainer and
kept confidential.

Maintainers will respond to reports and may edit, hide, or delete contributions, or block
participation, when this code is broken. Enforcement decisions are explained where doing so does
not expose the reporter.
